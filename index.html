Our Story

Born in Dublin. Inspired by Mexico.

El Grito started with one goal: bring authentic Mexican flavours to Ireland. Fresh tortillas, bold salsas and food made to share, served in a room that feels like a party the moment you walk in.

The name is a shout of joy. So come hungry, bring friends, and be loud about it.
